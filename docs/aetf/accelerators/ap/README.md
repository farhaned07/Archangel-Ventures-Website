# AP Accelerator

**Intelligent Invoice Processing for Microsoft 365**

AP Accelerator is the first Archangel accelerator. It turns invoice handling into a clear, repeatable process that finance teams can use inside Outlook, SharePoint, Teams, Power Automate, Dataverse, and Power BI.

## What it does

- Receives invoice emails
- Stores documents in SharePoint
- Extracts invoice data with AI Builder
- Validates against business rules
- Sends approvals in Teams
- Posts approved invoices to the ERP system
- Routes exceptions to finance review
- Logs everything for audit and reporting

## End-to-end flow

```text
Email arrives
  ↓
SharePoint archive
  ↓
AI extraction
  ↓
Validation
  ↓
Teams approval or exception
  ↓
ERP posting
  ↓
Audit log and dashboard
```
