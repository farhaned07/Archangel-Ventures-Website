## What Archangel does

Archangel works with management teams to:

1. identify expensive or repetitive work,
2. redesign the underlying workflow,
3. build and deploy the right AI system,
4. measure the operating result.

The current commercial focus is AI transformation in Thailand, supported by applied AI product and engineering work.
